## 🔍 Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset using SQL and Power BI to understand revenue trends, customer behavior, and churn patterns.

As a second-semester AI-ML student, my goal was to apply foundational SQL skills to solve real business questions and build a structured analytics workflow.

##  Tech Stack
- SQL (MySQL)
- Python (Pandas) - for inital data cleaning
- Power BI - for dashboard visualization

##  Revenue Analysis
- How is revenue trending over time?
- What is the average order value?
- Which days generate the highest revenue?

##  Customer Analysis
- Who are the top customers by revenue?
- How many repeat vs one-time customers are there?
- How are customers distributed by spending level?

##  Behavior Analysis
- At what time do customers place the most orders?
- Are customers more active on weekdays or weekends?
- How does order activity vary over time?

##  Churn Analysis
- What percentage of customers churn?
- How much revenue is lost due to churn?
- Which customers are at risk of leaving?

##  Key Insights
- -> Revenue shows a steady trend with fluctuations, indicating possible seasonal demand.
- -> A small group of customers contributes a large share of total revenue.
- -> Most orders occur during specific hours of the day, suggesting optimal times for promotions.
- -> Repeat customers play a significant role in overall revenue.
- -> Churn impacts a noticeable portion of revenue, highlighting the importance of retention.
